Hong Kong company

Hong Kong is one of the world's most important international financial, industrial and commercial services and shipping centres, and the Heritage Foundation has selected Hong Kong as the world's freest economy for 24 consecutive years, with excellent public security. Social integrity, simple tax system and sound legal system are well-known in the world, with the reputation of "Pearl of the Orient", Hong Kong's perfect legal system, simple tax system, low tax rates, complete infrastructure and communication facilities, high-quality professionals, favored by investors around the world. Hong Kong is a platform for the global economy with unlimited business opportunities, and Hong Kong's advantages are widely known, with tens of thousands of international companies benefiting from setting up companies in Hong Kong. 


Advantages of Hong Kong companies

[1] High visibility, easy to obtain brand effect;

[2] Business scope, very few restrictions, follow-up services are simple;

[3] The registration fee is small and no capital verification is required;

[4] No exchange controls, free movement of funds, free conversion of various foreign currencies;

[5] The legal system is sound and has access to international credit and financing;

[6] Logistics in and out without tariff;

[7] Low tax and low tax, report tax once a year;

[8] Double taxation protection treaties with multiple countries


Requirements for Incorporation of a Hong Kong Company

[1] One or more shareholders and directors, shareholders and directors are not subject to nationality restrictions

[2] All shareholders and directors must be over 18 years of age

[3] All shareholders must present a valid ID card or passport photocopy and proof of address

[4] If the enterprise is a shareholder, the enterprise must submit a copy of the business license and the identity card of the legal representative

[5] The minimum registered capital paid by the issued shareholder is HK$1

[6] The registered office can be provided in Hong Kong with a registered address or a secretarial company address

[7] Appoint a licensed company or Hong Kong resident as the statutory secretary

[8] Appoint a licensed company or Hong Kong resident as the designated representative



Time required to incorporate a Hong Kong company

[1] An individual as a shareholder may use electronic registration, which takes only 1 working day from the receipt of the properly signed file;

[2] As a shareholder, the enterprise needs to use manual channels, and it takes 6 working days from the receipt of the properly signed file

[3] Purchase of a ready-made Hong Kong company, it will take 2 working days from the receipt of the properly signed documents


Information required to incorporate a Hong Kong company

1. The company name to be used, preferably in Chinese and English, please provide 2-3 alternative company names

2. Determine the registered capital

3. Proportion of shares or number of shares held by each shareholder (e.g. more than one shareholder)

4. Photocopy of the identity of shareholders and directors and passports

5. Confirm the fiscal year

6. A brief description of the main business of the company to be incorporated

The identification and address documents of the above shareholders and directors need to be verified by our staff, notary office, lawyers, accountants or bank directors.
