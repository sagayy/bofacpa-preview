Company Profile

BOFA CPA Limited is a Hong Kong accounting firm that intends to provide services to clients around the world. Since its inception, BOFA has expanded its business coverage from Hong Kong to all parts of the world in this short period of time, and has set up offices and accounting firms in Singapore and the United States.

BOFA's services are extensive, including: providing professional services such as investigation and research, project planning, preliminary preparation, company formation, tax planning, accounting, auditing, legal advice, human resources, intellectual property rights, business agency, visa and immigration for customers to enter the target market and operate and maintain.

As a newly established professional brand, the structure is relatively simple and there is no bureaucracy of a large company, and with an experienced, exquisite business, young and energetic professional team - we can focus on our customers, we can provide tailor-made and well-defined services; in the face of customer requirements and problems, we are responsive and strong in execution, and can give back and deal with them in a short period of time.

BOFA has always been an important agent for long-term cooperation between many customers in Hong Kong, Singapore, cayman Islands, the United States, the British Virgin Islands (BVI) and other overseas regions, and is also a supplier to meet the needs of customers' overseas companies in local business registration, auditing, tax filing and other matters.
